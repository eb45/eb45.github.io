creating jekyll post directories (part 3)

February 03, 2021

What do you do when you want two post directories so only select posts show up on certain places on your site: Post directories.

See an example of this in my Series/Comp Sci Tutorials and in my Posts.

The easiest way to do this? Let’s imagine a scenario. You want one page to display posts about Topic A and the other page to display posts about Topic B.

  1. Put .markdown posts of Topic A in a folder called posts1 and the others in another folder called posts2.
  2. Within the _includes files (where the html of how your individual pages are generated resides), add a line to the top that specifies where you want your page to get its posts from.
